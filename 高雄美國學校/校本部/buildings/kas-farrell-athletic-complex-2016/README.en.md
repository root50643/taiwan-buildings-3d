Nominal1:300, millimeter units. Includes the high gym, dining low wing, roof sport platform and rear kitchen/mechanical projection. Four different elevations and shallow pitched high roof are reconstructed from architect-supplied drawings and2016completion photos. Height17.4m is published; all footprint/detail dimensions are graphic-scale estimates, not surveyed dimensions.

The adjacent Phase I academic building, sport field/boundary wall, buried pool and machinery, interiors, lettering and later changes are excluded. Glazing is opaque printable backing; small metal details are simplified/thickened. No physical trial print. Source images are not redistributed; see [credits and rights](SOURCE-RIGHTS.md), [parameters](parameters.json), and [QA](QA.json).
