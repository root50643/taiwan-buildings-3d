This report compares the authored model with the acquired official sources. All original source photograph pixels and native BF–4F streams, plus the full survey page renders, were inspected before geometry. ## Comparison outcome

- **Plan topology:** the authored `footprint.svg` and top render preserve two elongated lower wings and an elevated connecting central mass. Front and rear space between the wings is open above the display base. No rectangular building infill covers those courts. No basement U-footprint is projected through the upper floors. Gray 4F wing areas were not converted into roof terraces.
- **Front (`front`, `front-detail`):** compared with both campus enlarged front PNGs, General Affairs photos 1/2 and PDF physical p12 top photo. Four cylindrical piers support a projecting broad beam; the dark entrance sits behind the porch and above broad steps. Two levels of white screen families and upper dark slots / red panels distinguish the four-level central front. Mauve is confined to the photographed front family, not blanket-painted over unseen faces. Detailed glazing/window sizes and count, step count, rail dimensions and exact canopy depth remain approximate. No photograph pixels, gold text, banners or electronic sign text were copied.
- **Foreground oblique (`source-side`, `oblique`):** compared with the actual native PDF p12 image105 and official long-side photos3/4. The foreground wing has three visible floor bands, a pale end with simple dark windows, long recessed outer galleries, pale frame/eaves, red parapet family and a pitched/gabled roof family. Gallery depth is real geometry, not a flat black texture. The triangular gable infill is pale; sloped roof surfaces carry muted purple-red. Thirteen piers / twelve regular bays are an authored simplification, **not a counted source claim**. The distant wing is treated as plan-connected mass; unrelated tall background buildings are omitted.
- **Both long sides (`right`, `left`):** the matched side receives supported gallery-family detail. The opposite side intentionally lacks invented mirrored window arrays and remains neutral closed mass with minimal floor bands; this is a disclosure of missing evidence, **not a claim that the real wall is blank**.
- **Rear:** neutral closure; no unsupported rear windows, roof equipment, stair tower, chimney or lift housing. Source-backed colors are not extended indiscriminately to unknown surfaces.
- **Top:** roof family and mass hierarchy are visible. The higher central roof is a simple neutral rectangular cap, explicitly hypothetical; it is not presented as the actual roof construction, a verified terrace or a complete drainage/ridge reconstruction. Wing pitches, rear end shapes, and roof junctions remain inferred. The white skeletal source element is omitted as unresolved.

## Important visible compromises

This is a low-detail exterior model. Solid red panels replace unprintable tiny lattice holes. All exterior dimensions and proportions are approximate design units. Unknown surfaces intentionally look under-detailed. The model does not reconstruct interior plan partitions, complete basement exposure or slope/topography. The thin gray-green base is a display aid only. Images are source-direction comparisons, not camera-calibrated photogrammetric overlays.

## QA caveats

- Mesh and 3MF checks validate digital artifact structure, not architectural fidelity.
- 300 horizontal sections and morphological width screening do not establish a certified minimum global wall thickness; sharp corners/roof tips and small features remain scale-sensitive.
- Gallery slab undersides, canopy, louvers and eaves require printer-specific bridging/support review. No slicing toolpath, G-code or physical FFF print was produced.
- Gallery frame connections use positive overlap. The exported STL was rebuilt and reloaded, then passed all reported edge and vertex link checks.

Original source checksums were rechecked after modeling and pass unchanged.
