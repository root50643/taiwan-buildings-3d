# Visual comparison and bounded interpretation

The author inspected all seven original floor guides (1F–6F and B1), all seven architect photographs, the city daytime and nighttime exterior images, the interior context image, and the north-up roof aerial before authoring. The final nine rendered views were then inspected individually.

## Observable matching features

- **East / front:** a broad pale portal and deep horizontal canopy; blue-green upper glazing with two broad intermediate piers; long orange-brown vertical middle screen; pale middle ledge; lower double-height entrance glazing and an offset open northeast area. The six documented above-ground levels do not become six uniform strips.
- **North / northeast:** a projecting glazed bay near the east corner, a recessed tall brown-screen zone and a separate slender west-end glazed projection with a cap. This face is not a mirrored south elevation. Lower areas unseen through trees are reduced to neutral envelope surfaces.
- **South / southeast:** a long brown fin field, pale horizontal ledges, lower glazed zone, genuinely open horizontal roof pergola, and a taller southeast pale core with a narrow blue-green strip. Fin counts, mullion widths, intermediate bands and exact core proportions are simplified.
- **West / roof-oblique:** visibly different upper and lower outlines and west-side indentations remain. Unknown facade detail is neutral rather than fabricated windows. This is not a claim that the real west wall is blank.
- **Top:** the east canopy band, southeast core, north core and cap, west low roof rectangle, southwest shallow roof region, asymmetric envelope and central dark rectangle are present. The aerial is used for relative position, not roof height. The heights stay near the joint photograph silhouettes, with no additional full roof storey.
- **Central dark zone:** a shallow neutral top feature and rim. No full-depth open shaft, transparent roof, truss or occupied interior was inferred.

## Iterations actually made

1. The first east rendering showed that the 5F outline inadvertently covered the lower part of the upper glazing. The east-edge coordinates were corrected to the intended recessed upper face and new renders were checked.
2. The first south rendering hid the tall stair-window strip behind the core's small polygonal projection. Its backing and narrow colored strip were moved to the visible face within that projection, then the south and oblique renders were checked.
3. Independent exported-mesh checks caught one collinear zero-area roof/west-junction triangle. A 0.00001 mm manifold simplification removed it; the model was exported and rendered again. Final mesh/intersection tests refer to the resulting STL, not the rejected intermediate export.
4. Final front, southeast oblique, north, west, south, top, east detail, northeast and southwest roof-oblique PNGs were inspected. All nine are real model renders with no source pixels. The detail view intentionally crops the outer frame; oblique views prioritize the building rather than every display-plinth corner.

## Limits that remain

Guide drawings have no engineering dimensions; the model is not a measured plan registration or photogrammetric camera solution. Exact metrics, capture dates, west detail, roof covering type, hidden equipment, interiors and underground construction remain unverified or omitted. The small approach is illustrative and the base is a display device. The visible color families are uncalibrated and no inscription, logo, source-photo texture, foliage or streetscape is reproduced.
