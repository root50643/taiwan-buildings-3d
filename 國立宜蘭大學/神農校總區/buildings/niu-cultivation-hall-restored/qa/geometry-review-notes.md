# Geometry review notes

Final model SHA-256: `0827a741ec86c047708d5698c540d353a223347f25a1d883256b236bf495b70c`.

The final raw binary STL has 1,832 faces, 918 exact-coordinate vertices and 2,748 edges. All edges have exactly two oppositely directed uses; every vertex link is one cycle. There are no duplicate or degenerate triangles, and there is one face-connected component (Euler characteristic 2). Bounds are 166 × 111 × 54.4000015 mm; signed volume is 579,817.648514 mm³. No mesh repair was applied for these checks.

Both intersection implementations refined every one of 22,225 conservative AABB candidates from all 1,677,196 unordered triangle pairs. The floating implementation reports zero unexpected contacts. The second implementation converts original finite STL coordinates exactly to dyadic integers, uses unbounded integer signed-plane predicates and exact Fraction intersections, and also reports zero unexpected contacts. Its 12 synthetic controls include crossing triangles, coplanar containment, shared-edge area overlap, vertex-only contact, interior point/segment touch and near-coplanar separated triangles; all behave as expected. See `geometry-exact-collisions.json`. This is custom checked code, not a formally verified library or a certificate of printability.

All 272 horizontal layer-centre planes at 0.2 mm spacing have legal raw contour graphs and polygons, with one component and no holes. Section-integrated volume differs from mesh volume by +0.001163688%. Exact vertex-plane coincidences are nudged +0.00001 mm and logged in the CSV. These samples do not certify sub-layer or arbitrary-direction wall thickness.

The actual sections were opened and closed at 0.6, 0.8, 1.0 and 1.2 mm diameters. The deliberate 0.4 mm finger loses 1.6 mm², a 0.4 mm slot fills 1.6 mm², and the positive 2 mm finger remains intact at 0.8 mm. At 0.8 mm, the final model has three planes with loss above 0.01 mm², a maximum of 0.090615201 mm² (0.0007388703% of that section), and no entirely disappearing planes. The remaining largest loss at z=29.3 mm occurs at eight narrow ends of the photographed facade's arch recesses (x approximately ±51, ±55, ±61, ±65 mm; y approximately -37.5 mm). Other small losses arise along shallow recess/chamfer edges. They remain in the reported evidence.

At 1.0 mm opening, 138 planes lose more than 0.01 mm²; the maximum is 8.022175 mm². Actual difference polygons at z=20.5 mm include approximately 0.9 mm wide mullion footprints and the entrance's shallow subdivisions. These are detail-retention and strength limitations, not through-body wall failures; slicing and an actual print are needed to judge their final appearance. Detailed XY bounds are in `geometry-thin-feature-locations.json`. No universal minimum 3D wall thickness or successful physical print is asserted.

The initial pointed-ridge model is retained by the main workflow in `initial-tip-check`. Its 0.8 mm opening loses all 39.467198 mm² of the z=54.3 mm tip section and 17.727084 mm² at the entry tip. The final small ridge flats remove these two large losses. This manufacturing revision must remain distinguished from measured architectural evidence.

The first floating collision implementation produced 18 apparent near-coplanar intersections because it used the final contact tolerance to decide whether vertices lay on a plane. This broadened near-coplanar triangle/plane intersections into incorrect intervals. Those first results are retained in `geometry-initial-collision-findings.json`; original per-pair plane distances, normals and triangles plus exact classifications are retained in `geometry-initial-collision-diagnostics.json`. Separating arithmetic-zero and final contact tolerances corrects that implementation; a full exact-arithmetic recheck of the initial STL also finds zero unexpected intersections (`geometry-initial-exact-collisions.json`). No original flag was discarded as merely adjacency, and no tolerance was relaxed to force a pass.

The initial empty-Polygon drawing exception and the supplemental un-nudged-plane localization exception are retained in the execution history as QA tooling failures. They do not represent completed geometry passes. The final full workflow completed successfully. Geometry evidence alone does not establish support accessibility, slicer success, architectural fidelity, material strength or printer readiness.
