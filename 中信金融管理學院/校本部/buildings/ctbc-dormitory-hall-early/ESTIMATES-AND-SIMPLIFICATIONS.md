## Direct versus estimated
The official completion year and total floor area are direct source values. Every modeled length, height, setback, projection, level, wall/window dimension and curve is a photographic/plan/orthophoto estimate or manufacturing choice. Plan labels are low resolution and units unconfirmed; hypothetical64.5m axis chain is not a measured external length. After rejecting a mixed-roof68m starting line, main high-wing length62m, upper heightabout22.3m, room depth16m, east depth9m, northward short-wing heightabout21.4m and eastward extensionabout9m remain adjustable estimates. The main room rear step is about47.5m from the western datum. Envelope estimates refer to the principal masses, excluding local projecting plates, canopy edges and foreground arcade. Roof-edge relief displacement and uncertain grade can create larger errors than a rough±2m starting cue. No numerical accuracy guarantee is made.

## Appearance evidence
South long face: six architect photographs and design elevation, photographs prevail where design differs. West end: photographed corner and reverse-elevation end projection. East entrance: southeast photographs and plan. Main long-wing courtyard face: inferred from reverse design elevation only. North short-wing south face and east plates: partly visible in southeast photographs. North short-wing north face: no verified openings, intentionally simplified blank wall. Roofs: conservative original flat envelopes, no unproven equipment.

## Deliberate limitations
Exterior-only massing; no interior rooms, stairs, true structural shell or inaccessible basement reconstruction. Courtyard stairs, base/grade, sills, mullions and exposed steel are reduced or thickened for a small-scale solid model. Unknown surfaces stay simple rather than adopting mirrored windows. Early roofs exclude later photovoltaic arrays visible in contemporary orthophoto. The2022 renovation render's curved canopy is not treated as built or included. No neighboring building, vegetation, temporary elements or signage.

All downloaded photographs, architectural drawings, orthophotos and derivative crops remain private references in the separately backed-up research package; they are not model textures or public deliverables.
