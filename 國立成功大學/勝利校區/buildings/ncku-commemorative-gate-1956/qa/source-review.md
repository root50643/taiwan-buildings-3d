2026-10-20 21:50 UTC. Reviewer research_next_campus_structure. No model or main writes.

## Decision: HOLD for proportion revision, not identity/topology rejection
Personally viewed front, rear, elevation, top, left_oblique PNG files under ncku-gate-20261020/model/previews and compared west-pano.png plus east pub-002.png (pub-006 previously inspected).

PASS features: two white rectangular piers; single open passage; black waisted stepped xumi bases; five staggered relief rows; cloud rather than dot ornament outlines; genuinely recessed lower inscription beam; no invented back building. Plain cap top and omitted calligraphy are acceptable only as expressly disclosed simplifications. Front/rear equivalence without inscriptions reduces distinction, but is not a topology error.

Required proportion correction: inscription beam in current elevation is too tall relative to the cloud band and actual doorway. Current piers are also visually too broad relative to the passage. Together these make the gate read heavier and shorten the aperture. Current pier 28/152 = 18.4% body width. In near-frontal official photograph, visible frontal pier faces are approximately 14–17% of body width (perspective and tile stitching make this a range, not survey). Suggested first revision: piers 22–24 mm each, clear width 104–108 rather than 96. Keeping official approximate opening H:W=1:1.61 yields clear height about64.6–67.1 mm versus59.6; raise inscription-beam underside accordingly. Re-render elevation/front for visual comparison. These are model tuning values, not real-world dimensions.

## Ratio wording
Official VR wording: 整體高、寬比約為1:1.25，門洞在須彌座上方的高寬比則為1:1.61.
Official magazine p47 wording: 基座上方高度及寬度比例為1:1.61.
VR supports an opening interpretation more specifically; magazine is less precise and does not define exact dimension endpoints. Neither is a dimensioned survey drawing. It is therefore NOT justified to certify exact dimensional endpoints from these prose ratios. However, photo clear opening also appears wide rather than tall, so there is no reason to discard1.61 entirely. Current overly tall beam can be improved by a wider clear opening/narrower pier without contradicting approximate textual ratio. Do not label this the exact original ratio-derived geometry.
