# QA scope

Geometry checks: finite vertices, positive triangle areas, every edge exactly2 faces, closed manifold, consistent winding, one connected component, positive volume; binary STL exact triangle match;3MF vertex/triangle/material/unit match; Blender visible mesh match, no external images.

Visual/source checks: independent review of actual six render images against official floorplan, whole-building axon, front and long-side photos. Initial issues were corrected:7→6 side windows, free-side windows descend to0.8m, roof portal frames added, main hall length normalized from plan. Read visual-review.json for final scope/status.

No physical trial print, no slicing/support test; closed manifold is not manufacturing certification. Fine frame relief may need thickening for a specific printer. Historical sources do not certify2026 condition.
