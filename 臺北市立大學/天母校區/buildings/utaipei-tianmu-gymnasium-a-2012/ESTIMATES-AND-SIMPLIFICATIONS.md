# Evidence, estimates and simplifications

## What is directly supported

The selected building is University of Taipei, Tianmu campus, Gymnasium A, completed and in use in 2012. It is one model set. It is not neighbouring B/C gym buildings. The former Taipei Physical Education College identity is not counted as a separate present-day university.

The school presentation contains three plans and two sections, plus exterior photographs. The official project page identifies a three-storey steel building, 12,755 m², designed/supervised by Asian United Architects and constructed by Hwang Chang General Contractor. The operational presentation says June2012 opening; a city announcement says completed/in use July2012. We retain only their common supported year and do not invent a single month milestone.

The school presentation is a public operational introduction. Its plans/sections have no verified as-built stamp or issue/revision date. The model is a source-constrained exterior study, not a measured survey or a guarantee of the2026 condition.

## Roof and facades

- Main roof: a double-curved elliptical central surface with a continuous low rectangular skirt and rounded thick outer lip. Two orthogonal section silhouettes constrain the shape. The analytic curve and connecting surface are approximations, not a recovered engineering equation.
- Raised cap: curved top and underside, rounded plan corners, with an inset gray clerestory collar. Its support is not a full-width37×30 m box.
- Pink fascia: a vertical decorative band with a wavy lower edge. West/east short faces have three upward-opening lobes, the middle wider. The south long face has two broad lobes, verified by separating the southeast photograph at its corner.
- North: independently parameterized. The photograph constrains the near-B-stair entrance, rolling shutter, adjoining windows and a local dip/rise. Total north wave count, far-end details and exact transition coordinates are unverified. The rest is conservative plan-constrained approximation; it is not a verified mirror of the south.
- West entry: two external stair assemblies, recessed2F entry portals, central ground doors and tall gridded glazing. Window count, small frame widths, precise curtain-wall details and wall panel seams are simplified.
- Cardinal directions are inferred from plan/photo/orthophoto correlation. The published plan has no confirmed printed north arrow.

## Manufacturing changes

Nominal scale1:500,2 mm per estimated metre. The base is210×152×2.4 mm. With the−0.5 m outdoor grade and roof above1FL, overall model height is about63.8 mm.

Small mullions/transoms are widened to approximately1.04 mm. Stair guards replace thin real rail wires with1.2 mm thick solid guard/stringer panels, rooted fully to outdoor grade. Exterior stairs and platforms have solid undersides and are united with the base/building. These are print-scale substitutions, not claims about real member sizes.

The unmeasured cap-corner extrapolation is changed for manufacturing: four top corners rise by up to0.6 m (1.2 mm on the model) to join the27.8 m axis eaves, and the outer soffit is flattened at a minimum27.35 m. The curved central underside28.2 m, crown30.2 m,37.2×30.2 m projected outline and inset collar remain. This slightly changes visible corner silhouettes; it is not a verified roof detail. A blunt full-width lip avoids an acute bevel. Main roof lip thickness is also deliberately retained.

Fascia end returns have a flat0.8 m exposed run (1.6 mm on the model), and the central short-face wave crest is lowered0.22 m (0.44 mm) to retain about1.08 mm of material below the fascia top. The three/two lobe rhythms remain source-led. These manufacturing changes do not fill the main2F entry recesses.

Interior sports floors, seating, track, partitions and space trusses are omitted. The STL is a single exterior solid; slicer infill is still configurable. No internal room layout or structural-analysis model is implied.

Roof safety rail wire, small signage, unverified equipment and surroundings are omitted. Procedural color lines suggest panels; they are not measured panel inventories or tiny STL grooves. No source photograph is embedded as a texture.
