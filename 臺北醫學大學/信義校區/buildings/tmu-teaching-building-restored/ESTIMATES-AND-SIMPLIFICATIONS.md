# Estimates, evidence and authored manufacturing choices

## Direct source semantics
- Overall source length: 82 m, university 2024 report. Its measurement endpoints are unspecified. The main modelling bar is 82 m; projecting end shades and the attached elevator are authored outside it.
- Three storeys, 14 tiered classrooms. Original embedded escape plans support 4 + 5 + 5, two U-stair/service bands, ground-only covered passage, and one corridor-side elevator. The solid model does not claim to reproduce detailed classroom interiors.
- Historical storey height: 4.5 m. Multiplying by three gives approximately 13.5 m main roof datum, not a measured highest point or current set of floor elevations.
- Historical fin thickness: approximately 0.1 m. Do not drop the approximation.

## Estimated external geometry
- Chosen main depth approximately 16 m, within the approximately 16–18 m proportional evidence range. The original embedded plans were viewed directly; the distorted PDF page proportions were not traced.
- Main X bands: end classrooms at roughly 0–11.9 and 70.2–82 m, inner classroom/stair bands between. These are normalized plan estimates, not surveyed axes.
- Main NE grille/roof fascia limited to approximately X 11.9–70.2 m. Broad brick terminal walls retained. The stone arcade is limited further to approximately 18.7–63.4 m so straight first-floor bays remain below the grille at either end. Seven merged openings are an exterior simplification, not a source count.
- NW (inferred orientation) short face has about 1.55 m high shade fascia and recessed simplified windows. NE roof fascia is about 1.5 m high. These are photograph-led proportions, not dimensions stated by the architect.
- SW elevator X roughly 59.1–62.7 m, Y 18–21.5 m, estimated top 14.5 m. Short bridge envelope and solid edge rails are simplified. The independent forecourt clock tower is absent.
- Two grey roof activity patches and their paired solid rails are estimated positions/shapes. A local roof photo does not verify this symmetric arrangement. Roof machinery, roof stair exits and undated PV are not inferred.
- SE short face remains deliberately low-detail, with no verified detailed window inventory. No NW details are copied and claimed as SE evidence.

## Manufactured geometry, not as-built facts
- Nominal scale 1:400. Base 220 × 70 × 2.4 mm, overall Z approximately 38.65 mm.
- 77 merged NE fins, nominal 1.2 mm wide, instead of approximately 0.25 mm true-scaled historic fins of unknown exact count. 0.5 mm plan corner fillets and rear overlap into solid wall retain stout roots.
- Exposed SW columns and short brick wingwall plan corners are also filleted by 0.5 mm. Central stone arcade is deeper than the proportional starting draft, with a real rear overlap.
- Floor plates are roughly 1.375 mm thick in the print. Rails are merged solid raised edges. These selected feature values are not an asserted global minimum wall guarantee.
- U stairs are thick ramps and landings, with upper ends inside floor plates; fine treads are omitted. Manufacturing overlap is intentionally visible in the exported original surface, not added after QA.
- All images come from the exported original STL triangles in Blender with self-authored flat materials. No downloaded image texture, mesh repair, normal repair, face deletion, sample shift or QA tolerance change.

## Orientation
Local +X approximately NW, +Y approximately SW, +Z up, from campus/orthophoto/plan/photo correspondence. The source plans have no north arrow. Camera aliases: front=NE oblique, rear=SW oblique, east=NW end, west=SE end, south=NE elevation, north=SW elevation. True-bearing precision is not claimed.
