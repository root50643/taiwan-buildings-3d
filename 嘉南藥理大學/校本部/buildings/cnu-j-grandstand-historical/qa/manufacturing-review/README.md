# Manufacturing review and conditional printing requirements

The four unchanged, fixed print STLs have a conditional GO under the independent review requirements below. **The original automatic-bridge-direction main-body slice is not approved for printing.** Its successful slicer exit and the original broad manufacturing-pass claim do not establish a usable printing method. No physical print, support removal, dry fit, adhesive-strength, bending or transport test has been performed.

The original `geometry-review.json`, `slicing-summary.json`, logs, profiles and `slice-evidence.png` remain unchanged as historical diagnostic evidence. The original main-body profile and G-code are reference-only, superseded for printing guidance by the [independent review](../independent-support-review/INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md) and [slicing/support audit](../independent-support-review/slicing-support-audit/SLICING-SUPPORT-REVIEW.md). Neither the original nor the corrected diagnostic G-code is a recommended machine print file.

## Fixed geometry and assembly

- Inventory: 22 pieces, four unique geometries: body 1, canopy 1, column 10, upper mast/brace frame 10.
- Each exported assembly STL is closed, consistently wound, one connected component and positive volume. Manifold3D accepts each with `Error.NoError`.
- All 231 pairs of placed assembly pieces were intersected in their original coordinates. Maximum overlap is 0 mm³; failure threshold is 0.0001 mm³.
- Direct triangle-ray measurements on exported STL give brace widths 1.7999997–1.8000002 mm, frame side thickness 2.2000000 mm, column thickness 2.8000000 mm, canopy field thickness 2.4000015 mm, and 2.0000000 mm web between aligned upper/lower sockets.
- All 50 sockets were independently measured. Total X/Y key clearance is approximately 0.4 mm, or 0.2 mm per side, within STL float precision. Body key bottoms have a 0.30 mm gap; canopy key bottoms/ceilings have 0.15 mm gaps. Shoulders clear by 0.05 mm. These are clearance-fit adhesive positioning joints, not snap fits.

Print the body upright, canopy top down, and columns/upper frames on their flat side. Clean all body support, socket openings and elephant foot before assembly. Dry-align body → ten columns → canopy, then add the ten upper frames, respecting their different front and rear feet. This order leaves all sockets accessible and avoids threading braces through the canopy. Use material-compatible gap-filling adhesive and a temporary parallel jig after the unglued alignment check. The side-flat frame and column STLs retain a common flat face over their complete shapes.

The overview's 214 × 90 × 53.4 mm envelope is nominal. Natural dry seating at the shoulders lowers the columns by approximately 0.05 mm, canopy by a cumulative 0.10 mm, and upper frames by a cumulative 0.15 mm, giving approximately 53.25 mm maximum height. This is an interface-geometry estimate, not a measured physical fit. The assembled overview STL is not the manufacturing orientation and must not be printed as one piece.

## Required bridge direction and retained support

The main body has a measured 5 mm front balcony projection and needs support. At Z=12.6 mm, the original `diagnostic-main-support.ini` placed platform bridges along the long direction. Its longest bridge-labelled segment was approximately 179.28 mm, with approximately 174.289 mm of continuous centerline remaining unsupported after projected support bands from preceding object extrusion and nearby support extrusion were deducted. A successful exit and absence of warnings therefore do not approve the original method. The number is an extrusion-line/width support abstraction, not a measured 174 mm free-bridge failure.

The corrected [reference profile](../independent-support-review/slicing-support-audit/REVIEW-ONLY-main-short-axis-support.ini) uses `bridge_angle=90` and `dont_support_bridges=0`. In the saved orientation it bridges the platform along its short axis and retains support below bridges, reducing the same platform-layer maximum unsupported centerline interval to approximately **1.704001 mm**. Re-slice for the intended printer and inspect actual short-axis paths, support below bridges and every affected layer. Do not copy only the angle number: rotation or another slicer may change its meaning. The diagnostic profile has no valid machine start/end code.

The corrected body still has a maximum internal infill-top support gap of approximately **8.224913 mm**. At Z=1.8 mm, the canopy's 15% stars infill-top paths have approximately **8.036027 mm** gaps. The thirty bed-facing canopy sockets close at Z=1.0 mm with approximately **2.600011 mm** across their 2.6 × 3.8 mm openings; this is not the largest bridge in the part. Use material, cooling and flow settings already able to handle approximately 8.23 mm internal bridges. Otherwise increase infill or solid layers, re-slice, and inspect infill closure and socket shape. The canopy, column and upper frame diagnostic slices used supports disabled.

## Staged support removal before assembly

The original slice's 26,586 actual support-extrusion segments produced 83,924 unique samples at spacing ≤1 mm. Eight horizontal rays left 580 samples without a direct route; these original flags remain in the evidence. All 580 lie in two outward-open shallow windows behind the front stairs. Their three-leg routes first reach Y=−33, then X=±65, then Y=−60 without model obstruction. The wall/stair outer slots are approximately 4.5 mm wide and 9.4 mm high; the audit also records radius-0.5 mm axial samples.

The new short-axis profile was checked separately: **30,369 support segments and 95,890 unique samples**, with 95,310 having at least one direct horizontal exit. The remaining 580 are again in those two shallow windows and their sampled three-leg routes are clear. The new support reaches Z=23.4 mm. No added enclosed region was found in this finite check; the original and corrected flags and supplementary evidence are preserved separately.

This evidence supports only the geometric possibility of fine-tool access, staged fragmentation and lateral removal. **Do not pull the support out as one block straight toward the front.** The checks do not prove that a complete bent tool or captured support block can pass, and do not model other support blocking the route, adhesion or removal force. Clear the body before attaching columns, canopy and frames. If tools cannot safely turn into the approximately 1.2 mm-deep shallow windows, revise support placement or use compatible soluble support, then re-slice; do not force removal or enclose support during assembly. Actual removal remains untested.

## Diagnostic conditions and evidence limits

Diagnostic slicing used PrusaSlicer 2.9.6 at **100% size**, PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer height, nominal 0.45 mm line width, 3 perimeters, **15% infill**, generic 205/60 °C temperatures and a 250 × 210 mm bed. The 100% value refers to scale, not solid infill. These are reference conditions, not a machine-independent recommendation.

The four original unique-part slices exited successfully without warning/error/repair lines. Object extrusion heights are 140 / 18 / 14 / 11 layers at 0.2 mm for body / canopy / column / frame. The original body has 189 total layer-change records because independent support layers are interleaved; it still has 140 distinct object extrusion heights and reaches Z=28.0 mm. Each part used a separate new settings directory. These original results are retained for provenance and do not overrule the rejected main-body bridge direction. The independent audit separately preserves the corrected slice, profile/G-code hashes, commands, parser methods and its own failed runs and corrections.

`geometry-review.json` records raw coordinates, thresholds, measurements and all collision/clearance results. `slicing-summary.json` records the original STL/G-code hashes, commands, toolpaths, support bounds and layer evidence. `slice-evidence.png` shows the original balcony-support and canopy bridge paths, not a verified removal operation. Full logs and reproducible scripts/profiles are included. Executable diagnostic G-code remains outside the deliverable.

This review does not substitute for the separate triangle self-intersection QA. Caliper rays are located feature checks, not complete arbitrary-direction minimum-wall proof. Centerline support intervals are not thermal, sag or material simulations; support-access samples are not a comprehensive removability guarantee. The 192 × 60 mm canopy needs warping/bed-adhesion control and full cooling before bed removal. Thin frames may break during brim removal, handling or gluing. Physical support removal, fit, adhesive strength and machine-specific thermal/warping behavior remain unverified.
