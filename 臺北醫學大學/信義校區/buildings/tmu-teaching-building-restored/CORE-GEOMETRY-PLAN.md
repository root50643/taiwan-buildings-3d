# Source-constrained core

The model represents the TMU Xinyi Campus Teaching Building restored in 2021–2022, as evidenced in the 2023 municipal award publication and 2024 university article. MOE115 school code 1028. The forecourt clock tower is expressly excluded.

Local +X is approximately NW and +Y approximately SW, inferred by matching the three original embedded escape-plan images, campus location, orthophoto and photographs. The PDF slide pages were not traced because their embedded drawings are non-uniformly stretched.

The main source length is 82 m; the chosen depth is an explicit approximately 16 m estimate within the 16–18 m source-constrained range. Three historical 4.5 m storeys yield a calculated roof level of approximately 13.5 m. Neither floor elevations nor roof highest point are surveyed.

The five classroom bands at upper levels follow the original plan (end rooms, inner rooms, and central room), with two U-stair/service bands between. The central first-floor band is a through-passage only; its ceiling and 2F/3F classroom and roof remain intact. Interiors are solid mass rather than a surveyed detailed interior. The two U stairs are stout ramp/landing representations and do not imply roof access. A single estimated grey elevator and three short bridges project on SW near NW quarter. It is not the clock tower.

NE vertical fins begin at upper-floor height; SW has open horizontal corridors and terminal brick walls. NW short end has recessed windows, thick deep horizontal shade fascia and red brick wingwalls. NW identification is inferred from site relationships. The SE end is deliberately low-detail, with no unverified detailed window inventory.

The front stone arcade is confined to the evidence-supported central segment. Seven openings are a finite simplified rhythm, not a measured opening inventory. Straight 1F terminal areas remain. The entrance is shallow steps, not the historical bare giant canopy.

No source image is used as texture. No undated solar array, unverified roof equipment or neighboring building is added. Both roof activity-field outlines and the paired rail arrangement are explicit estimates, not claimed verified roof layouts.
