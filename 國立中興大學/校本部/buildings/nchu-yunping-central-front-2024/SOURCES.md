## Observed versus approximated
- Observed: white tall central rectangular facade, narrow vertical grille divisions, two distinct lower PV-covered sloped planes, smaller PV planes flanking red-brown tapered upper front, central entry and right ground windows.
- Modeled only central front slice: **not the whole building**. Left/right diagonal wings, full roof plan, unseen rear, interiors, roof equipment/open top and site context are omitted.
- Tower is only its visible front trapezoidal slice. Its shallow flat rear is an authored cut, NOT an asserted closed real tower.
- Bottom-left is tree-obscured: no conjectural windows/doors; blank supporting surface is artificial. Ground stairs are simplified; exact riser count and ramps not established.
- All dimensions, slope depths/pitches, fin thicknesses and colors are photo-informed approximations, not survey/as-built data. PV cells/seams are reduced symbolic grids; exact cell counts not claimed.
- State represented is the2024 source photograph, not verified current2026.
- Photos remain private research references outside this package; none are included or embedded.
- The fine diamond mesh behind the white fins is **not reproduced**: it is abstracted as dark flat glazing-like panels with reduced rectangular bands. This is a declared visual simplification, not evidence that the real grille is plain glass or rectangular mesh.
