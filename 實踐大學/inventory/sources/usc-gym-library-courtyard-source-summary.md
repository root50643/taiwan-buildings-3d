## 原包所列來源

- [ARTECH](https://www.krisyaoartech.com/en/projects/education/Shih-Chien-University-Gymnasium-and-Library)：Built facade photographs; model photograph for supporting topology only.
- [ARTECH-CATALOG](https://www.krisyaoartech.com/data/catalog/file/260416_web-EN.pdf)：Official building identity, completion date, stories and project data. 第24頁。
- [AD-PLAN-SECTION](https://www.archdaily.com/278300/shih-chien-university-gymnasium-and-library-artech-architects)：Published site plan and section, no numeric scale or elevations.
- [USC-MAP](https://www.usc.edu.tw/p/412-1000-97.php?Lang=zh-tw)：Building identifiers and current floor uses; not a dimensional survey.
- [USC-LIBRARY](https://lib.usc.edu.tw/p/412-1075-899.php?Lang=zh-tw)：Campus map and library identity.
- [NLSC](https://maps.nlsc.gov.tw/S09SOA/homePage.action?Language=ZH)：PHOTO2 z19 3x3 georeferenced roof/court imagery; roof parallax acknowledged.

## 精度、取捨與公開範圍

配置圖無數字比例尺，剖面無標高；水平外廓及高度為圖像比例估算。M主屋頂30m、N主屋頂29m與N東南屋頂27.8m是建模選擇，非實測標高。草坪六採光罩採建成證據，不照搬剖面八輪廓。孔網／格柵、樓梯、支承、屋頂與採光罩有列印加厚及簡化；地下兩層室內不建。

只收錄原創模型、模型自主渲染、程式及數值檢查資料與來源連結。原網站照片、PDF圖面及正射參照板的再散布權未確認，均不納入公開成品。
