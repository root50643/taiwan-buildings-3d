# Central entrance facade: visual scope comparison

The builder inspected actual pixels in the three official front photographs S1 p19, S2 p69 and S3 p22 figure 2, then the six model renders. This is a builder comparison, not independent acceptance.

- Rectangular lower recess and broad shallow-arched glazing retain negative depth and pale frames. The central wide upper opening plus two narrow slits on each side are present.
- Two pairs of round columns and a pale curved rail sit above the red masonry, with a directly visible gray front eave strip only. No side balcony or full cylinder is added.
- Lower entrance band stays gray; masonry is muted orange red; trim is warm pale; glazing is a dark opaque proxy. Brick/tile details are authored motifs, not copied pixels.
- Side and rear previews clearly show the straight artificial crop/backing. The top view exposes only the front eave strip and unmodeled space; no whole roof or courtyard claim is made.
- Approximate proportions, 1 mm-thick rail/mullion simplifications, unknown photo dates, unseen roof/rear omissions and the non-surveyed display dimensions are disclosed in README.
- No source images, logos, sign imagery, source textures or third-party CAD/mesh are included.

No calibrated color comparison, metric photogrammetry, surveyed-scale validation, or physical print was performed.
